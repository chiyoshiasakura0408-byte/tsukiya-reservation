Cancellation Policy

As we select and prepare ingredients especially for each reservation, the following charges apply to cancellations and changes.

Cancellation fees

From 3 days before your visit through the day of your visit: 100% of the course price.
No-show: 100% of the course price.

Changes to guests or date

From 3 days before the reservation, a reduction in guest count or a date change incurs 100% of the course price for the affected guests. Please call us as soon as possible if your plans change.

Late arrivals

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late without contacting us, your reservation may be treated as cancelled.
