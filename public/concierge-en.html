The philosophy of Tsukiya

We explore how deeply we can bring out the flavour of crab. Origin, season and each individual crab shape its character. We choose the finest live crab available and honour classic preparations—sashimi, boiled and grilled—while exploring new possibilities through sweetness, umami, aroma and texture.

Our omakase celebrates crab from beginning to end. Pairing crab miso with truffle is intended to reveal another dimension of the crab miso through harmony between the ingredients. Each dish offers a different expression of crab.

Time to savour nothing but the flavour.

We carefully remove the shells so that you can enjoy the aromas, textures and delicate flavours without the work of shelling. A few dishes intentionally retain their shells to bring out another aspect of the crab's appeal.

Our commitment to live crab

Live crab means crab that is still alive. After death, digestive enzymes in the crab miso and other processes rapidly reduce freshness. Even as a crab weakens, compounds responsible for umami are consumed and its original flavour begins to fade.

We prepare the crab after welcoming our guests. Time after preparation can bring darkening, loss of resilience in the flesh, and the loss of precious flavour with moisture. This requires considerable effort, but we consider it essential to preserving the crab's umami, aroma and texture.

We take great care during transport, bringing live crab to the restaurant by air or in live-fish transport vehicles.

A distinctive taste. Tsukiya's crab.

At the restaurant, we let the crab rest in our tanks for half a day to a full day, allowing it to recover from transport before serving it at its best.

Equipment using patented technology removes excretions and secretions, while ozone sterilisation helps maintain a clean seawater environment. Careful conditioning brings out rich, creamy crab miso with fewer unwanted flavours and aromas, and flesh with the supple resilience of live crab.

We do more than keep the crab alive: we bring it into the best possible condition. That extra care supports the flavour of Tsukiya's crab.

Planning your visit

Reservations & payment

Our sittings begin at 18:00 and 20:30, Japan time. The booking page supports 2–8 guests, subject to availability.

Full advance payment for the course is required to confirm an online reservation. Please follow the payment instructions on the reservation page and in your email. Payment is due within 48 hours. Payment is accepted by credit card only. Unpaid requests are automatically cancelled. The two winter Matsuba courses include beverages, with some drinks charged separately. Additional charges are settled at the restaurant.

Counter & private rooms

Choose the counter or a private room when booking. Private rooms are in the Bettei annex, a separate building from the main restaurant. Please check the venue in your confirmation email.

Main restaurant: 3-10-4 Nishitenma, Kita-ku, Osaka.
Bettei private rooms: 3-8-7 Nishitenma, Kita-ku, Osaka.

Allergies, celebrations & special requests

Please tell us about allergies or dietary requirements before reserving. Our team must confirm whether we can accommodate your request. Cakes, flowers and other arrangements also require confirmation and may incur additional charges.

Use the enquiry form below, or add a note when making your reservation.

Changes & cancellations

Cancellations from 3 days before your reservation through the reservation day, and no-shows, incur 100% of the course price. From 3 days before the reservation, reductions in party size incur 100% of the course price for the reduced number of guests; date changes are also subject to this policy. Please review the full policy before confirming your reservation.

Sending an enquiry does not cancel or change a booking. Please await confirmation from the restaurant.
