Planning your visit

Reservations & payment

Our sittings begin at 18:00 and 20:30, Japan time. Online reservations are available for 2–8 guests, subject to availability.

Full advance payment for the course is required to confirm an online reservation. Please follow the payment instructions on the reservation page and in your email. Payment is due within 48 hours; unpaid requests are automatically cancelled. Beverages are paid for at the restaurant.

Counter & private rooms

Choose the counter or a private room when booking. Private rooms are in the Bettei annex, a separate building from the main restaurant. Please check the venue in your confirmation email.

Main restaurant: 3-10-4 Nishitenma, Kita-ku, Osaka.
Bettei private rooms: 3-8-7 Nishitenma, Kita-ku, Osaka.

Allergies, celebrations & special requests

Please tell us about allergies or dietary requirements before reserving. Our team must confirm whether we can accommodate your request. Cakes, flowers and other arrangements also require confirmation and may incur additional charges.

Use the enquiry form below, or add a note when making your reservation.

Changes & cancellations

Cancellations from 3 days before your reservation through the reservation day, and no-shows, incur 100% of the course price. From 3 days before the reservation, reductions in party size incur 100% of the course price for the reduced number of guests; date changes are also subject to this policy. Please review the full policy before confirming your reservation.

Sending an enquiry does not cancel or change a booking. Please await confirmation from the restaurant.
